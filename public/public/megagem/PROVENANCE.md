# Actual blog figure provenance

Generated figures and compact source tables for `docs/actual_blog.md`. Quantitative inputs were rechecked on 2026-07-25. `make_blog_figures.py` renders the SVGs with the Python standard library; no quantitative value comes from an AI-generated illustration.

`scripts/analysis/fetch_modal_blog_evidence.py` downloads a fixed allowlist from the read-only `megagem-results` Modal volume. `scripts/analysis/build_blog_evidence.py` verifies every SHA-256 hash and writes normalized CSVs. The raw 33 MB snapshot stays in a temporary audit directory; `source_manifest.json` records its 63 remote paths, hashes, sizes, and retrieval time.

## Reconciliation notes

- The seven mainline GRPO configurations are a reconstructed audit set, not a persisted seven-run manifest. Only seat rotation and the heterogeneous run record direct frozen-step-0 SFT anchors. The weak-KL run is a later eighth ablation. Missing anchor measurements are never encoded as zero.
- The 24-game seat-rotation heuristic comparison is a seam diagnostic (`K=1`, below its planned sample) and its source marks it invalid as final evidence. “Pass” in the article refers only to optimizer health.
- The current W&B credential exposes no matching `megagem-phase3` project or run metadata. Older custom-step logging also omitted later roll records for at least one run. Canonical Modal `phase3_grpo.json`, `train_log.json`, and evaluation sidecars therefore supply the figures; W&B is not used.
- The long-DAPO checkpoint gains +16.48 own-score points [14.26, 18.93] over SFT against the scripted heuristic but fails its optimizer-health gate because maximum KL reaches 0.646. Entropy, KL, and reward telemetry diagnose optimization, not transferable skill.
- The archived simulator fails its absolute-level calibration gate, and nominal baseline estimates drift across packets. Arms with $\lambda\ge1$ exploit a simulator-only proxy-rescue channel and are muted. The archived $(0.5,2)$ setting is shown in the recovered sweep, but live evaluation supplies the effect estimate.
- The control-variate adjustment assumes value-weighted calibration, which no persisted artifact independently validates. The article keeps raw paired estimates primary and treats adjusted estimates as model-assisted corroboration.
- The SFT-fitted price law retains about 91% of the clairvoyant-value offline regret improvement over logged SFT bids and 81% of the adjusted live effect. Those are different denominators and are labeled separately. The 91% value comes from the archived June 10 analysis document because its standalone offline summary is not present in the pinned raw snapshot.
- The June 10 document reports an older point-EV regret of 3.613. The newer frozen revalidation gives point-EV 3.275, distributional-EV 2.819, and SFT regret 4.124; the blog and figure use that packet.
- The held-out intervention ladder is not cumulative. Each row compares one treatment with its run-specific SFT control. Raw paced margin is +12.16 [7.61, 16.71]; the displayed adjusted estimate is +12.62 [8.28, 16.96].
- The balanced 13-model BIBD supplies the broad Elo chart. The separate top-three test contains 450 games in 150 deck clusters and is used only for the focused Distilled/Pro/Flash comparisons in the article.

`dynamics_sweep.csv`, `live_selector_checks.csv`, `offline_regret.csv`, `pikl_arms.csv`, and the four `selfplay_*.csv` tables are regenerated from pinned JSON. `frontier_leaderboard.csv`, `liquidity_ledger.csv`, and `winning_progression.csv` are document-derived publication tables: the builder hashes them but does not reconstruct them.
